Who We Are

The TEC Spartans program is an exclusive, student-focused esports and gaming ambassador community. We empower students passionate about gaming to become campus leaders and industry trailblazers.

  • Organize Events: Lead and execute gaming events on your campus.
  • Real Campaigns: Participate in and drive real campaigns for top industry brands.
  • Build Community: Help cultivate and grow the gaming culture among your peers.
  • Gain Experience: Develop practical skills in event management, content creation, and networking.
